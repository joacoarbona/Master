Methodology & sources

Macro figures are drawn from Wayra / Telefónica official communications and corporate profiles (Wayra.com, Telefónica press room, Crunchbase, PitchBook, Tracxn, CB Insights). Aggregate counts vary by source and date: Telefónica/Wayra cite €245M+ direct investment (plus >€1.9B from third parties) across 1,200+ startups with a ~530-company active portfolio; PitchBook tracks ~1,096 investments and 629 exits across the broader group; Tracxn records 4 unicorns (Airalo, Perplexity, Devo + 1), 3 IPOs (incl. Flywire) and 17 acquisitions on its tracked subset.

Company-level entries (value proposition, founders, country, stage, status) are compiled from public sources. Founders and exit details should be re-verified before any external citation.

On returns: the only portfolio-level performance figures Wayra has stated publicly are directional — e.g. at its 2021 ten-year milestone Wayra reported its CVC investments had appreciated ~70%+ in value with 75+ successful exits, and Wayra Brasil/Vivo Ventures report startups generating hundreds of millions of reais in contracts with Vivo. Per-deal returns (IRR/MOIC/exit values) remain undisclosed.

Scope note: No public source publishes the complete deal-by-deal Wayra ledger with full attributes. This explorer holds a representative, fact-checked subset spanning every hub (Spain, UK, Germany, Brazil, Wayra Hispam, Wayra X, Wayra Ventures) and every lifecycle stage.
