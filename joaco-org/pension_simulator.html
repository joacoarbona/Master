Step 00 · The Why

North Star & Zero-Based Reset

A restructure is a rare permission to start from zero. Nothing the old team did carries over by right — every activity must re-earn its place against the new mandate. This is where we name the single outcome BPO is accountable for.

Mission — the one sentence
Enable the Oncology Business Unit to plan, decide and execute with speed and confidence — turning strategy into coordinated, measurable action.
The four zero-based questions
01
If BPO didn't exist, would we invent it?Justify the function's existence against the new OBU remit — not its history.
02
What outcome do we own that no one else does?Find the irreducible mandate. Everything else is shared or out.
03
What would we stop doing today?De-scoping is the value of zero-basing. Name what falls away.
04
With limited capacity, what comes first?A new team can't do everything at once. Sequence ruthlessly.
The shift this restructure must deliver
Reactive reporting→Forward-looking decision support
Fragmented planning across teams→One integrated planning rhythm
Data scattered in silos→A single, trusted source of truth
Tracking activity→Managing outcomes & performance
Ad-hoc requests & firefighting→A predictable operating cadence
Manual, retrospective analysis→AI-augmented ways of working

Step 01 · The What & How

Remit, Boundaries & Operating Model

Zero-basing the scope: draw three lines — what BPO owns outright, what it co-owns (and so needs explicit decision rights), and what it deliberately puts down. The hardest column is the third.

Core BPO owns

  • Integrated business planning (annual + LRP)
  • The operating rhythm / business cadence
  • Performance management & KPI framework
  • Forecasting process & assumptions governance
  • Resource & budget allocation process
  • Commercial project & portfolio governance
  • Ways of working, playbooks & standards

Shared co-own · RACI

  • SFE — targeting, segmentation, territory (w/ Sales)
  • Incentive compensation (w/ Finance & HR)
  • Data governance & analytics (w/ DS&AI)
  • Market & competitive intelligence (w/ Marketing)
  • Demand signals & access inputs (w/ Medical, MA)
  • Above-country reporting alignment (w/ Global)

Out of scope de-scoped

  • Day-to-day brand marketing execution
  • Individual rep coaching & field management
  • IT system administration
  • Raw data engineering / pipelines (DS&AI · IT)
  • Regulatory & medical content
  • One-off bespoke analysis on demand

Step 04 · The Tactical Plan

Roadmap, Governance & Metrics

Prioritized initiatives sequenced into three phases. Stabilize first to earn credibility, build the engine, then scale and embed. Governance is the rhythm that keeps it honest; metrics tell you if it's real.

Stabilize
0–3 MONTHS
Earn trust. Get the basics running and decision rights clear.
· Operating cadence / business review rhythm · Roles, RACI & decision rights · Stakeholder map & comms plan · KPI framework v1 · Resource allocation framework · PMO / portfolio governance basics
Build
3–9 MONTHS
Construct the engine — integrated planning, forecasting, ops.
· Integrated annual planning redesign · LRP / forecasting model & process · SFE refresh: segmentation & targeting · Incentive comp design & ops · AI use-case backlog (w/ DS&AI) · Capability building / playbooks
Scale
9–18 MONTHS
Mature the data foundation and embed AI ways of working.
· Single source of truth maturity · Self-serve analytics / AI planning assistant · Embed AI into the operating rhythm

Operating cadence — the governance rhythm

Weekly

Ops stand-up

Issue triage, blockers, in-flight initiative status. Short, action-only.

Monthly

Business review

Performance vs plan, KPI deep-dive, decisions on the table.

Quarterly

Strategic review

Re-prioritize the matrix, course-correct, resource reallocation.

Annual

Plan + LRP

Full integrated planning cycle and long-range refresh.

How we'll know it's working

Leading we control

  • Cadence adherence — % of reviews held on schedule
  • Planning cycle time — from kickoff to signed plan
  • Data trust score — stakeholder confidence in numbers
  • RACI clarity — % of decisions with a named owner
  • # AI use-cases live in the planning workflow

Lagging the outcome

  • Forecast accuracy vs actuals
  • Decision speed & quality (leadership rated)
  • OBU performance vs plan
  • Stakeholder satisfaction with BPO
  • Reduction in ad-hoc / off-cadence requests
A word on honestyThis plan is a starting hypothesis built from a generic OBU BPO pattern — validate the remit, owners and scores with your own leadership before committing. The structure is sound; the specifics are yours to challenge.
